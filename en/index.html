01 Expertise

Casting engineering, not generic advice

I work at the intersection of process, tooling and production economics. Every task is framed in numbers: scrap, yield, cost per good unit.

01.1

Low-pressure die casting

Silumins, wheels, radiators: thermal conditions, feeding, process stability and batch-to-batch repeatability.

01.2

High-pressure die casting

Dies, parameters, defects: cold shuts, porosity, shrinkage. I address the cause, not the symptom.

01.3

Tooling & simulation

Die and gating design, ProCAST, 3D scanning, thermal analysis before the first shot.

01.4

Product launch · APQP / ANPQP

From concept to series: plans, prototypes, validation, documentation and preparation for Renault-Nissan and other OEM audits.

01.5

Quality & risk · FMEA

PFMEA, root-cause analysis, CpK, 8D, statistical process control and IATF 16949.

01.6

Scrap & cost

Loss diagnostics, material rationalisation, yield improvement and lower cost per good unit.
